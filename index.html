Style

Australian Style — Short length, braided edges, diagonal cut bottom center, bolo cord sides, wide V neck. 2 front pockets + 1 back pocket + neck strap.
NZ Style — Medium length, straight cut bottom, bolo cord or buckle sides and shoulders, V neck. Same options as Australian with NZ-specific proportions.
American Style — Longer cut, side slits in side panels, button placket up front, removable back embroidery backing.
Euro Style — Mid to full length, straight bottom edge, centred zipper, closed sides, comfortable fit.
Tactical Style — Short length, rounded cut bottom, open sides with six utility buckles, round neck. Built for utility.
SWAT Style — Based on bulletproof vest shape, not actual armor. Short length, utility pockets, Velcro front. Built for club/fashion use.
